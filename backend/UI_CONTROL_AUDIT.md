## UX observations

The current visual hierarchy and spacing are coherent on desktop and phone. At 390 px, all nine screens and the lead modal fit without document-level horizontal overflow. Lead tables have an internal horizontal scroll with a visible hint. The new mobile Sections menu exposes all nine destinations without requiring a swipe; every route, active label, outside click, Escape, and keyboard focus were tested. Secondary metadata remains compact, but the updated typography is legible at native 390 px screenshots.

## Reproduced issue and repair

Before repair, `Leads & research → open lead → Manage follow-up tasks →` changed the underlying route but kept the lead modal open. Tasks were blocked. The main agent changed hash navigation to close the lead dialog. The exact click path passed in visible Edge afterward, as did Close and Escape.

## Limits

This covers all safe browser actions reachable in the isolated fixture states. It does not establish working live Maps collection, external website access, AI summaries, email delivery, phone calls, public hosting, or provider credentials. Credential dependent actions were kept separate from simulated provider tests.
