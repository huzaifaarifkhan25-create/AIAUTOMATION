# What this project does

AIAutomation is a workspace for finding med spa prospects and planning automation services. It was built because collecting business details, checking websites, judging opportunities, and remembering follow-up work are scattered across Maps, spreadsheets, notes, and messaging tools. The app brings those steps into one workflow.

## How to use it

Open **Discover & import** and enter a city such as “Islamabad, Pakistan”; the app searches for medical spas there. You can also enter a full business-and-location query. **Start live pilot** uses the Edge collector to inspect up to ten Maps listings. Edge opens each place to capture details and closes when the run ends. A successful run saves source pages and a CSV, then shows the count and preview. Nothing is imported automatically. Check every name, category, address, and contact detail; Maps can return massage spas or unrelated businesses. Choose **Import** only for relevant results. **Open Google Maps** is a manual search link and saves nothing. A prepared CSV can also be previewed and imported.

After import, use **Leads & research** to inspect public website evidence and unknowns. The ranking separates publicly supported prospect fit from confirmed operational need. **Follow-up tasks** and contact history organize manual work. Workflow drafts describe possible automations; **Automation lab** can run supported reminders in a local sandbox and show unsent previews. Client setup and operations screens support reviewed local activation and monitoring.

## What to do next

Start with a small, specific search and preview its category and source evidence. If a pilot fails, read its saved reason and try another query or CSV import. Validate the proposed service with real med spa operators before claiming a confirmed need or business result. Live email, calls, external workflow execution, public hosting, and individual user accounts require separate setup and verification.
